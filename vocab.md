[Vacant]

- Meaning (EN): empty; not occupied
- Word Origin: 来自拉丁 vacare，意为「空着、未被占用」。词根 vac- 表示空，同根词有 vacuum（真空）、vacation（假期）。
- Related Word: occupied — 已被占用
- Sentence 1: The bus had a vacant seat by the door.
- Focus 1: vacant seat
- Sentence 2: We found a vacant room at the hotel.
- Focus 2: vacant room

[Proximity]

- Meaning (EN): the state of being near; closeness
- Word Origin: 来自拉丁 proximus，是 prope（近）的最高级，意为「最近的」。同根词 approximate（接近）里也藏着它。
- Related Word: distance — 距离；远隔
- Sentence 1: Our school is in close proximity to the library.
- Focus 1: in close proximity to
- Sentence 2: I like the apartment’s proximity to the subway.
- Focus 2: proximity to

[Devise]

- Meaning (EN): to plan or invent something carefully
- Word Origin: 经古法语 deviser（安排、规划）来自拉丁 dividere，意为「分开」。把整体拆成可执行的步骤，就引申出「设计、想出」。
- Related Word: improvise — 临场即兴、不预先规划（devise 重在事先谋划，improvise 重在不做准备当场发挥）
- Sentence 1: We devised a plan for the class trip.
- Focus 1: devised a plan
- Sentence 2: She devised a simple way to save time.
- Focus 2: devised a simple way

[Mundane]

- Meaning (EN): ordinary and not interesting
- Word Origin: 来自拉丁 mundus，意为「世界」。原指属尘世的东西，和神圣相对；世俗久了，就变成「平凡的、乏味的」。
- Related Word: extraordinary — 非凡的、不同寻常的
- Sentence 1: Washing dishes feels like a mundane task.
- Focus 1: mundane task
- Sentence 2: His office job became a mundane routine.
- Focus 2: mundane routine

[Translucent]

- Meaning (EN): allowing some light to pass through; semi-transparent
- Word Origin: 来自拉丁 trans（穿过）+ lucere（发光）。光能穿过但看不清后面，就是半透明。同根词有 lucid（清晰的）。
- Related Word: opaque — 完全不透光的（transparent 全透 / translucent 半透 / opaque 不透，三个词是一条梯度）
- Sentence 1: Sunlight came through the translucent curtain.
- Focus 1: translucent curtain
- Sentence 2: The bathroom door has translucent glass.
- Focus 2: translucent glass

[Probe]

- Meaning (EN): to investigate or examine closely
- Word Origin: 来自拉丁 probare（检验、证明）。原义是外科用的探针，后引申为「探查、深入调查」。同根词有 prove、approve。
- Related Word: glance — 匆匆一瞥、只看表面（与深入探查相反）
- Sentence 1: The interviewer tried to probe deeper into his answer.
- Focus 1: probe deeper
- Sentence 2: Police will probe the cause of the fire.
- Focus 2: probe the cause

[Revive]

- Meaning (EN): to bring back to life or make active again
- Word Origin: 来自拉丁 re（再）+ vivere（活）。让东西重新活过来。同根词有 vivid（鲜活的）、survive（幸存）。
- Related Word: decline — 衰退、走下坡（与恢复活力相反）
- Sentence 1: The poster helped revive interest in the club.
- Focus 1: revive interest in
- Sentence 2: Rain may revive the dry plants by morning.
- Focus 2: revive the dry plants

[Inhabit]

- Meaning (EN): to live in a place
- Word Origin: 来自拉丁 in（里）+ habitare（居住），而 habitare 又源自 habere（持有），即长期待在一个地方。同根词有 habit（习惯）、habitat（栖息地）。
- Related Word: vacate — 腾空搬离（和 Vacant 那张卡互相呼应）
- Sentence 1: Many fish inhabit the river.
- Focus 1: inhabit the river
- Sentence 2: Only a few families inhabit the island in winter.
- Focus 2: inhabit the island

[Credibility]

- Meaning (EN): the quality of being believable or trustworthy
- Word Origin: 来自拉丁 credere（相信），加上 -ible（可…的）和 -ity（性质），字面就是「可被相信的性质」。同根词有 credit、incredible。
- Related Word: distrust — 不信任（可信度归零之后的态度）
- Sentence 1: He began to lose credibility after he lied.
- Focus 1: lose credibility
- Sentence 2: The false story hurt the website’s credibility.
- Focus 2: website’s credibility

[Lucid]

- Meaning (EN): clear and easy to understand
- Word Origin: 来自拉丁 lucere（发光）。原义是明亮的，后引申为思路清晰、容易懂。和 Translucent 同根。
- Related Word: obscure — 晦涩难懂的
- Sentence 1: The teacher gave a lucid explanation of the rule.
- Focus 1: lucid explanation
- Sentence 2: She gave a lucid answer to the question.
- Focus 2: lucid answer

[Fatigue]

- Meaning (EN): extreme tiredness after long effort
- Word Origin: 来自拉丁 fatigare（使疲惫），经法语 fatigue 进入英语，拼写几乎没变。
- Related Word: energy — 精力、活力
- Sentence 1: After the long game, mental fatigue made it hard to focus.
- Focus 1: mental fatigue
- Sentence 2: By 10 p.m., fatigue set in and everyone grew quiet.
- Focus 2: fatigue set in

[Impulse]

- Meaning (EN): a sudden urge to do something without thinking
- Word Origin: 来自拉丁 im（朝着）+ pellere（推）。一股把内心推向行动的力量，就是冲动。同根词有 compel（迫使）、expel（驱逐）。
- Related Word: restraint — 克制、自制
- Sentence 1: I bought the snacks on impulse near the cashier.
- Focus 1: on impulse
- Sentence 2: Stores put candy there to encourage impulse buys.
- Focus 2: impulse buys

[Recede]

- Meaning (EN): to move back; to gradually become smaller or weaker
- Word Origin: 来自拉丁 re（回）+ cedere（走）。往回走、退下去。同根词有 concede（让步）、proceed（前进）、precede（在前）。
- Related Word: advance — 前进、推进
- Sentence 1: The lights receded into the distance as the car drove away.
- Focus 1: receded into the distance
- Sentence 2: After some rest, the pain receded.
- Focus 2: pain receded

[Distorted]

- Meaning (EN): changed in shape or meaning; twisted or unclear
- Word Origin: 来自拉丁 dis（分离）+ torquere（扭）。扭得变形，就是扭曲、失真。同根词有 torture（折磨）。
- Related Word: accurate — 准确的、不失真的
- Sentence 1: The old speaker made a distorted sound.
- Focus 1: distorted sound
- Sentence 2: The mirror gave a distorted view of the room.
- Focus 2: distorted view

[Deception]

- Meaning (EN): the act of tricking someone by lying or hiding the truth
- Word Origin: 来自拉丁 de（向下）+ capere（抓），合成 decipere，意为把对方抓住骗走。同根词有 capture（抓住）、perceive（察觉）。
- Related Word: honesty — 诚实
- Sentence 1: The trick was an act of deception.
- Focus 1: act of deception
- Sentence 2: The scam used deception to get money.
- Focus 2: used deception

[Prosperous]

- Meaning (EN): successful, wealthy, or doing well
- Word Origin: 来自拉丁 prosperus（顺遂的），传统解释为 pro（依照）+ spes（希望），即顺着希望发展。同根词有 prosper、prosperity。
- Related Word: struggling — 挣扎度日、经济困难的
- Sentence 1: A prosperous town usually has many busy shops.
- Focus 1: prosperous town
- Sentence 2: The family became prosperous after years of work.
- Focus 2: became prosperous

[Elusive]

- Meaning (EN): difficult to find, catch, or understand
- Word Origin: 来自拉丁 e(x)（出）+ ludere（玩），合成 eludere，意为戏耍着溜走，让人抓不住。同根词有 illusion（幻象）、delude（欺骗）。
- Related Word: obvious — 显而易见的、不必费力捕捉
- Sentence 1: An elusive answer kept bothering the class.
- Focus 1: elusive answer
- Sentence 2: The small cat was elusive and hard to catch.
- Focus 2: was elusive

[Bloated]

- Meaning (EN): swollen; or made too big with unnecessary parts
- Word Origin: 来自古诺斯语 blautr，意为软的、泡涨的。先指身体浮肿，后引申为机构、预算臃肿。
- Related Word: lean — 精瘦的；精简的（身体和预算两个义项都能对上）
- Sentence 1: The project started with a bloated budget.
- Focus 1: bloated budget
- Sentence 2: I felt bloated after dinner.
- Focus 2: felt bloated

[Contempt]

- Meaning (EN): strong disrespect; the feeling that someone is beneath you
- Word Origin: 来自拉丁 con（完全）+ temnere（轻视）。全然看轻一个人，就是轻蔑。
- Related Word: respect — 尊重
- Sentence 1: He spoke with open contempt about the rule.
- Focus 1: open contempt
- Sentence 2: She looked at the bully with contempt.
- Focus 2: with contempt

[Centennial]

- Meaning (EN): relating to the 100th anniversary of something
- Word Origin: 来自拉丁 centum（百）+ annus（年）+ -al。百年一次的。同根词有 century（世纪）、percent（百分之）、annual（每年的）。
- Related Word: annual — 每年的（百年一次与一年一次的对照）
- Sentence 1: The school held a centennial celebration in May.
- Focus 1: centennial celebration
- Sentence 2: We saw a centennial sign in the hall.
- Focus 2: centennial sign

[Atrocity]

- Meaning (EN): an extremely cruel or brutal act
- Word Origin: 来自拉丁 atrox，意为凶残的、骇人的。atrocity 是它的名词形式。
- Related Word: mercy — 仁慈（与残暴相反）
- Sentence 1: The documentary described a war atrocity.
- Focus 1: war atrocity
- Sentence 2: Everyone was shocked by the atrocity.
- Focus 2: by the atrocity

[Phantoms]

- Meaning (EN): ghosts; shadowy figures (real or imagined)
- Word Origin: 来自希腊语 phantasma（幻象），经古法语进入英语。同根词有 fantasy（幻想），都出自「显现」这个希腊词根。
- Related Word: reality — 现实（幻影的对立面）
- Sentence 1: He still felt phantom pain in his leg.
- Focus 1: phantom pain
- Sentence 2: She heard phantom sounds in the quiet room.
- Focus 2: phantom sounds

[Negligence]

- Meaning (EN): failure to take proper care; carelessness
- Word Origin: 来自拉丁 neg（不）+ legere（挑选、关注），合成 negligere，意为不去关注。同根词有 neglect（忽视）、select（挑选）。
- Related Word: diligence — 勤勉、尽责（拉丁语里 negligentia 和 diligentia 就是一对反义词）
- Sentence 1: The report called it gross negligence.
- Focus 1: gross negligence
- Sentence 2: The family made a negligence claim after the fall.
- Focus 2: negligence claim

[Complicity]

- Meaning (EN): involvement in a wrongful act, especially by helping or allowing it
- Word Origin: 来自拉丁 com（一起）+ plicare（折叠），根义是缠在一起、被卷入同一件事。同根词有 complex（复杂的）、complicate（使复杂）。
- Related Word: innocence — 清白、无辜（与卷入罪行相反）
- Sentence 1: Silence can become complicity in bullying.
- Focus 1: complicity in bullying
- Sentence 2: He denied complicity in the cheating.
- Focus 2: complicity in the cheating
